# Marketing with AI by Tom Babb

A collection of skills — documented, repeatable workflows — for marketers building with AI.

Most AI marketing content teaches you what's possible. This repo teaches you how to do it. Each skill is a process I've tested, refined, and use. The goal is to give you something you can pick up and run with, not a framework you have to interpret first.

---

## Who this is for

Marketers, founders, and operators who are building with AI and want their workflows codified into something reusable. People who are tired of starting from scratch every time. People who believe the next moat in marketing is distribution — and that AI is how you build it faster than anyone else.

---

## How to use this repo

Each skill lives in its own folder under `/skills`. Every skill has a `SKILL.md` file that explains:

- What the skill does and when to use it
- What you need to start
- The step-by-step workflow
- Tools and resources
- Notes from real use

Read the skill. Follow the steps. Adapt where needed. Add your own.

This repo grows as I document more of what I'm doing. If you're using these skills and have feedback, find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tbabb02/) or [X](https://x.com/TBabb02).

## The skills

### Distribution

**[Reference, Scrape, Repurpose](skills/knowledge-scrape-to-content/SKILL.md)**
The internet is full of raw material most people scroll past. This is the system for turning what already exists online into a content strategy, a lead list, and a distribution engine.

Three skills make up this system:

| Skill | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| [Knowledge Scrape to Content](skills/knowledge-scrape-to-content/SKILL.md) | Scrape Reddit threads, YouTube videos, and forums for pain points and insights. Turn those into blogs that rank. |
| [People Scrape to List](skills/people-scrape-to-list/SKILL.md) | Find high-intent people in public corners of the internet. Enrich them into a contactable list with personalization context. |
| [Long-Form to Content Pipeline](skills/long-form-to-content-pipeline/SKILL.md) | Take one podcast, video, or essay and extract a month of content from it across written and video formats. |

### Brand

**[Brand from References](skills/brand-from-references/SKILL.md)**
Build a brand visual system and social media content in under an hour using an inspiration library and Claude Design. Start from a vibe. End with a design system you can build on indefinitely.

**[Brand Operating System](skills/brand-operating-system/SKILL.md)**
Convert a brand guide into a set of reusable prompts — one for voice, design, messaging, and identity — that any AI tool can use to produce on-brand output without a designer in the loop. Future brands won't have brand guides. They'll have prompts.

---

### Prompting

**[Constraint Prompting](skills/constraint-prompting/SKILL.md)**
Generic prompt → generic output. This skill fixes that. Build prompts that separate context (what the AI needs to understand) from constraints (what the AI must never do). Includes the five labeled slop patterns and specific fix prompts for each.

**[Reverse Prompting for Image Generation](skills/reverse-prompting-for-image-gen/SKILL.md)**
Start from an existing image, describe it in detail, use that description as your generation prompt. Produces dramatically better results than writing prompts from scratch. Includes the subject + setting synthesis technique for placing any subject in any environment.

---

### Content & Video

**[AI Video Production](skills/ai-video-production/SKILL.md)**
Three tools, three stages: Reve (text-to-image) → Veo 3 via Google Flow (image-to-video) → Adobe Premiere Pro (editing). The decision framework for when to stop at each stage. Don't start with video. Start with image.

**[Personal Content Engine](skills/personal-content-engine/SKILL.md)**
Build a full content distribution system around a single person — LinkedIn, podcast, voice clone, short-form video. Turn a subject matter expert into a content presence that compounds. Every audience can have its own spokesperson.

---

### Discoverability

**[GEO Optimization](skills/geo-optimization/SKILL.md)**
SEO optimized for Google. GEO optimizes for LLMs. As AI becomes the first place people go for recommendations and answers, your content needs to be readable by agents, not just humans. This skill covers authority, structure, discoverability, and conciseness — and how to write for an audience that doesn't assume anything.
