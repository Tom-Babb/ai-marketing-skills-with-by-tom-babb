# Marketing with AI by Tom Babb

A collection of documented, repeatable workflows for marketers who are building with AI.

Most AI marketing content tells you what is possible. This repo tells you how to do it. Each skill is a process I have tested, refined, and use regularly. The goal is to give you something you can pick up and run with on the same day you find it.

---

## This is for people who are done starting from scratch

Marketers, founders, and operators who want their workflows codified into something reusable will get the most out of this. If you believe the next moat in marketing is distribution, and that AI is how you build it faster than anyone else, this is the right place.

---

## Each skill folder has everything you need to run it

Every skill lives in its own folder under `/skills`, and every skill has a `SKILL.md` file that covers what the skill does and when to use it, what you need to start, the step-by-step workflow, the tools involved, and notes from real use.

Pick a skill, follow the steps, and adapt where your situation calls for it. This repo grows as I document more of what I am doing. If you are using these skills and have feedback, find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tbabb02/) or [X](https://x.com/TBabb02).

## The skills

### Distribution

**[The internet is full of raw material most people scroll past](skills/knowledge-scrape-to-content/SKILL.md)**

This is the system for turning what exists online into a content strategy, a lead list, and a distribution engine. Three skills make up this system:

| Skill | What it does |
|-------|-------------|
| [Knowledge Scrape to Content](skills/knowledge-scrape-to-content/SKILL.md) | Scrape Reddit threads, YouTube videos, and forums for pain points and questions, then turn those into blogs that rank. |
| [People Scrape to List](skills/people-scrape-to-list/SKILL.md) | Find high-intent people in public corners of the internet and enrich them into a contactable list with personalization context attached. |
| [Long-Form to Content Pipeline](skills/long-form-to-content-pipeline/SKILL.md) | Take one podcast, video, or essay and pull a month of content from it across written and video formats. |

---

### Brand

**[Build a brand visual system in under an hour](skills/brand-from-references/SKILL.md)**

Using an inspiration library and Claude Design, you can go from a rough direction to a full design system the same day. Start from a concept and end with something you can build on indefinitely.

**[Future brands will have prompts, not PDFs](skills/brand-operating-system/SKILL.md)**

This skill converts a brand guide into a set of reusable prompts, one for voice, one for design, one for messaging, and one for identity, so any AI tool can produce on-brand output without a designer in the loop every time.

---

### Prompting

**[A generic prompt will always produce generic output](skills/constraint-prompting/SKILL.md)**

This skill fixes that by separating context, which is what the AI needs to understand, from constraints, which are the rules that prevent known failure modes. It includes the five labeled slop patterns and specific fix prompts for each one.

**[Describe an existing image and use that description as your prompt](skills/reverse-prompting-for-image-gen/SKILL.md)**

Working backwards from a real visual reference produces far better results than writing image prompts from scratch. This skill includes the subject and setting synthesis technique for placing any subject into any environment.

---

### Content and Video

**[Three tools and three stages for producing AI video](skills/ai-video-production/SKILL.md)**

Reve handles text-to-image, Veo 3 via Google Flow handles image-to-video, and Adobe Premiere Pro handles editing. The skill includes a decision framework for when to stop at each stage. Start with image, not video.

**[Every audience can have its own spokesperson](skills/personal-content-engine/SKILL.md)**

This skill builds a full content distribution system around a single person, covering LinkedIn, podcast, voice clone, and short-form video. It takes a subject matter expert and turns them into a content presence that builds over time.

---

### Discoverability

**[LLMs are now an audience your content has to reach](skills/geo-optimization/SKILL.md)**

GEO, Generative Engine Optimization, is what SEO becomes when AI models are the first place people go for answers. This skill covers authority, structure, discoverability, and conciseness, along with how to write for an audience that never assumes anything.

Note: This skill is actively being expanded with more tactical workflows.
